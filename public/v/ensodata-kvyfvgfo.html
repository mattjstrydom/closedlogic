EnsoData: a Google Ads conversion fires when the demo page opens

Justin, this is the check we mentioned in our email. We looked at what ensodata.com sends to Google Ads, from outside your site, on 3 November 2026. No logins, no access to your accounts, and we didn’t touch or submit your form.

Here’s what we found, and the fix is below.

What we checked

  • Your Google Ads tag (AW-800077532) and your live Google Tag Manager container (GTM-P9CWBZM).
  • What the browser sends to Google Ads when someone opens /request-a-demo/.
  • The same on your homepage.

The main finding: a conversion before the form

  1. Opening /request-a-demo/ sends a Google Ads conversion (event page_view, label mVOFCKq78t8bENztwP0C) on page load, before anyone fills in the form.
  2. It comes from a page-view rule in your Google Ads tag for www.ensodata.com/request-a-demo/, the kind of URL-based conversion that’s set up in Google Ads rather than in Tag Manager. Your homepage sent no conversion.

Your Google Ads tag has a similar page-view rule for www.ensodata.com/ensohst (label Qgb7CK-68t8bENztwP0C), and your container has a conversion on page load for /ensosleep-ppg/ (label pVG8CN3Xo_8ZENztwP0C). We read those two from your tag files; we didn’t open those pages.

What it means for your ad spend: if that action is set as primary, Google Ads counts a visit to the demo page as a conversion, whether or not a demo is ever requested.

Evidence

The request your site sent to Google Ads when we opened /request-a-demo/, before any click:

GET https://www.googleadservices.com/pagead/conversion/800077532/
  en=page_view
  label=mVOFCKq78t8bENztwP0C

What we can’t see from outside

Which of these conversions are primary (used for bidding) and which are secondary (reporting only). That’s the one thing that decides whether this matters, and it’s a quick check in your account. We also ran the test from outside the US, so a cookie banner could behave differently for your US visitors.

Check it yourself in 2 minutes

  1. Open a new private window in Chrome, with any ad blocker turned off.
  2. Open developer tools (F12, or Cmd+Option+I on a Mac) and go to the Network tab. Tick Preserve log.
  3. In the filter box, type conversion/800077532.
  4. Open https://www.ensodata.com/request-a-demo/. Within a few seconds, a request appears with label=mVOFCKq78t8bENztwP0C in its URL, before you touch the form.
  5. Then in Google Ads, go to Goals, then Conversions, then Summary, find the action with that label in its tag details, and check whether it’s Primary or Secondary.

For your developer: the fix

  1. In Google Ads, set the demo page view action (mVOFCKq78t8bENztwP0C) to Secondary, or remove it. Count a demo request only when the form is actually submitted, and keep that as Primary.
  2. Because this rule lives in Google Ads, not in Tag Manager, it’s changed in the conversion action’s settings in Google Ads.
  3. Test it in 2 minutes: repeat the check above. Opening /request-a-demo/ should send no request with that label.
