Tapcheck: Google Ads conversions fire on a scroll and after 60 seconds

Katie, this is the check we mentioned in our email. We looked at what tapcheck.com sends to Google Ads, from outside your site, on 3 November 2026. No logins, no access to your accounts, and we didn’t touch or submit your form.

Here’s what we found, and the fix is below.

What we checked

  • Your live Google Tag Manager container (GTM-KZ2PDTP2) and the Google Ads account it sends to (AW-580064789).
  • What the browser sends to Google Ads when someone opens /get-a-demo, scrolls, and stays on the page.
  • The same on your homepage, with no scroll.

The main finding: conversions from scrolling and time on site

  1. On /get-a-demo, a Google Ads conversion (label g3RyCMm41M8cEJWszJQC) fired as soon as we scrolled halfway down, before any form was touched. Its trigger is a 50% scroll on any page.
  2. Another (label g03UCPOd1M8cEJWszJQC) fires after 60 seconds on any page. We saw it at about 60 seconds on /get-a-demo and on your homepage, including a visit where we didn’t scroll at all.

A third conversion (label K3ncCJvMypQdEJWszJQC) fired at the same 60-second mark. Its trigger is the 60-second timer and the 50% scroll together. Your container also has separate conversions for real HubSpot form submits, so these are counted alongside demo requests, not instead of them.

What it means for your ad spend: if either of the first two is set as primary, Google Ads is learning from scrolling and time on site, not just from demo requests, and the cost per conversion it reports will look lower than the cost of a real demo request.

What we can’t see from outside

Which of these conversions are primary (used for bidding) and which are secondary (reporting only). That’s the one thing that decides whether this matters, and it’s a quick check in your account. We also ran the test from outside the US, so a cookie banner could behave differently for your US visitors.

Check it yourself in 2 minutes

  1. Open a new private window in Chrome, with any ad blocker turned off.
  2. Open developer tools (F12, or Cmd+Option+I on a Mac) and go to the Network tab. Tick Preserve log.
  3. In the filter box, type conversion/580064789.
  4. Open https://www.tapcheck.com/get-a-demo and scroll slowly past halfway. A request appears with label=g3RyCMm41M8cEJWszJQC in its URL.
  5. Leave the page open without clicking. At about 60 seconds, another appears with label=g03UCPOd1M8cEJWszJQC.
  6. Then in Google Ads, go to Goals, then Conversions, then Summary, find the action with that label in its tag details, and check whether it’s Primary or Secondary.

For your developer: the fix

  1. In Google Ads, set the scroll (g3RyCMm41M8cEJWszJQC), 60-second (g03UCPOd1M8cEJWszJQC) and combined (K3ncCJvMypQdEJWszJQC) actions to Secondary, or remove them. Keep your HubSpot form-submit conversions as Primary.
  2. In GTM-KZ2PDTP2, pause the three Google Ads conversion tags that use the 50% scroll trigger, the 60-second timer and the trigger group, if you don’t need them for reporting.
  3. Test it in 2 minutes: repeat the check above. Scrolling and waiting 60 seconds should send no request with those labels.
