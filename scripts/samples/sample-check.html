A Google Ads conversion fires before anyone asks for a demo

We looked at what example.com sends to Google Ads, from outside the site. No logins, no access to the accounts, and we didn’t submit the form.

What we checked

  • The live tag manager container (GTM-XXXXXXX) and the Google Ads tag it loads (AW-XXXXXXXXXX).
  • What the browser sends to Google Ads when someone opens the demo page and clicks on it.
  • The fields and script on the demo form.

The main finding

Opening the demo page sends a Google Ads conversion (event page_view, label XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX) before anyone touches the form. The form-submit conversion is a separate action with its own label.

If the page-view action is Primary, Google Ads bids toward people who open the page, not people who ask for a demo, and the cost per demo it reports looks lower than it is.

Your 5-minute self-check

  1. In Google Ads, go to Goals, then Conversions, then Summary.
  2. Set the date range to the last 30 days and add the All conv. column.
  3. Compare the page-view action with the form-submit action. The ratio shows how inflated the count is.
  4. Check whether the page-view action is Primary or Secondary.

The fix

Set the page-view action to Secondary, keep the form submit and booking as Primary, and fire the submit conversion only on a confirmed submit. A pipeline stage is only a good bidding goal if the account has enough monthly volume for it.
