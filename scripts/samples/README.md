# Unpublished samples

Not deployed (outside `public/`). `sample-check.html` is the redacted version of the Borderless check. `sample-fix.html` is an illustrative Signal Fix handover.

## Left out of the sample check because it could still identify the company

- The job ad, its quotes and its date.
- All screenshots (the form, the Ads Transparency page) and the number of ads on record.
- The exact page path, the cookie banner behavior and its hidden form, the RevenueHero booking tool, and the Meta and LinkedIn tags. The mix is distinctive.
- The count of conversion actions on the path, the real form GUID, tag IDs and labels, the check date and time zone.
- The follow-up timing (the email, and the Head of Marketing start).
- The HubSpot embed type combined with the booking tool.

Even redacted, a reader who knows the company may recognize the pattern (a page-view conversion on a demo page). Matt to review before any publish.
