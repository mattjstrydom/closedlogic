Your 5-minute self-check

  1. In Google Ads, go to Goals, then Conversions, then Summary.
  2. Set the date range to the last 30 days and add the All conv. column.
  3. Compare the conversions on the page-view or click action ({{ACTION_NAME}}: {{PAGEVIEW_COUNT}}) with the form-submit action ({{SUBMIT_COUNT}}).
  4. The ratio shows how inflated the count is. If the page-view or click action is Primary, bidding is working toward visits, not requests.

The fix

Set the page-view and click actions to Secondary, keep the form submit and booking as Primary, and fire the submit conversion only on a confirmed submit. {{FIX_DESCRIPTION}}

A pipeline stage is only a good bidding goal if the account has enough monthly volume for it. If it doesn’t, form submit or booking stays Primary.
