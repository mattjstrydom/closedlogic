The problem

Three ways Google Ads ends up chasing the wrong people

Smart bidding optimises for whatever you tell it is a conversion. If that signal is wrong, so is every bid.

01

A page view counts as a demo

If opening your demo page or clicking a link counts as a conversion, Google Ads learns to find visitors, not buyers.

What it costs: a cost per demo that looks fine while sales sees nothing.

02

The click ID never reaches HubSpot

If the Google click ID isn't stored on the contact, the lead can't be matched back to the ad that brought it.

What it costs: you can't tell which campaigns make pipeline.

03

Bidding stops at the form fill

If qualified stages never reach Google Ads, bidding can't tell a real lead from a form sales threw away.

What it costs: budget keeps flowing to junk leads.
